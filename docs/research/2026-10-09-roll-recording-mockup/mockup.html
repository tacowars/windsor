Roll recording: Rec and Quantise

Today's Roll device with the song Keys I loaded (A natural minor, a 4-bar loop of 41 notes in an 8-bar region), plus a Rec switch and a Quantise button (dashed outlines mark them; the toggle above hides the outlines). The decisions behind them are in docs/log/2026-10-09-roll-recording.md. The Rec toggle steps through its states, the Snap and Selection toggles show how Quantise reads, and View switches between the rack device and Expand.

The proposal

  • Quantise sits in the first column under Fold, beside Snap, whose step it snaps to. Its label names the step (to 1/16), and the button names what it acts on: All notes, or 3 selected when notes are selected. With Snap set to Off it is disabled and the label reads snap off. One press is one undo step.
  • Rec sits in the second column under Audition, drawn as Audition's switch, with the record colour in place of the part colour. Its label reads the state:
    • Disabled: the switch dims, and the hint under it says why (No region under the playhead). The mockup imagines the playhead parked outside this part's regions.
    • Armed, stopped: On is tinted with its dot lit, and the label reads armed. Playing only sounds the part until the transport runs.
    • Recording: On is filled, its dot blinks, and the label reads recording. The playhead runs, and a held G4 grows from just after bar 3's downbeat (raw, as played: recording never quantises), drawn with a record-coloured edge. When its key is let go, just before bar 4, it is written and drawn as any note; the next pass plays it back.
    • Region full: at 2048 notes, the summary line on the device's top edge says so in the record colour, and the held note is not drawn: nothing more is recorded into this region.

Stand-ins

  • The Rec states are drawn on the captured song; "Disabled" and "Region full" are staged (the region covers the whole song, and it holds 41 notes, not 2048).
  • Only the toolbar and the Snap select respond. The captured controls are pictures of today's.
  • The roll refits its time axis to the window's width as the console's Fit does, from the captured positions.
