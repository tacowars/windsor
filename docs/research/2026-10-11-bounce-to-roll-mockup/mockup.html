Bounce to Roll

Today's Song view detail pane for the Grid part Acid Saw (the acid audition song: 16 steps at 1/16, A natural minor, 2 regions of 2 bars), plus a Bounce to Roll button in its header. A dashed outline marks the button; the toggle above hides it. The decisions behind it, and the render, the loop search and the Roll's new slide, are in docs/log/2026-10-11-bounce-to-roll.md.

The proposal

  • Placement. The button follows the summary note, 16 px clear of it. It is the console's btn at the size of the device's own Randomize and Reverse, 22 px tall like the fold arrow, so the header row keeps its height. After the fold arrow shows the other candidate, and Today, no button is the header as the build draws it, for comparison.
  • Recommended: after the note. The part's name keeps its place, which is where it is on every pane, including a chord's, which has no fold arrow. The command sits beside the summary it rewrites (grid · 16 steps · 1/16 · 2 regions), and it stays clear of the fold arrow, which is pressed far more often. Next to the fold arrow, a missed fold would rewrite the part. Undo takes that back, but it is a fright.
  • States.
    • Grid part: enabled, as on any Euclid, Grid, Chord, Arp, Basslead or Figure part.
    • No regions: drawn disabled, titled No regions to bounce (hover it). The pane shows today's hint in place of the device.
    • Roll part: no button. The header reads as the bounced part would: Acid Saw — Roll over roll · 12 notes · 1 bar loop · 2 regions.
    • Bounced: the pane right after the bounce, with the summary toast (an info toast): Bounced to Roll: 24 notes in 2 regions · 8 accents lost their mod. Those are the numbers the record works out for this part: each 2-bar region loops 1 bar of 12 notes, and 4 of them are accented, with an accent mod of 1. One undo step, "Bounce to Roll", takes it back.
    • Note cap: the bounce changes nothing, and an error toast says why. It stays until dismissed.

Stand-ins

  • No regions and Note cap are staged on the captured part, which has 2 regions of 12 notes each. The error toast's text is the record's example, not this part's.
  • The Roll part's device is not drawn: it is today's Roll device, unchanged, and a dashed box stands in for it.
  • The toasts sit at the window's foot on the right, where the console puts them.
  • Only the toolbar responds. The captured controls are pictures of today's.
