Master section makeover

The Mixer tab's master level, meters and output stage, redrawn in the insert rack's style. The meters move on a simulated drum loop, not real audio. Try the options, drag the Level fader and the Ceiling knob, and switch to the Song tab to watch the meters stop painting while the top-bar light keeps working.

Chosen on 2026-09-30: B · Side column, segmented meters, the bridge when the column scrolls away (on narrow screens, where the column stacks on top), and one meter bridge from the output stage's own In and Out peaks with the separate master tap dropped. The ✓ marks them in the toolbar; A and C stay here for reference.

What changes

  • One meter bridge instead of two. Today the master's L/R meter and the output stage's In L/R read almost the same signal: the master output, before and after the Music fader. The mockup keeps the stage's own In and Out peaks and drops the separate master tap, so the page runs one less audio worklet while it is open.
  • The master reads as a chain. Inserts, then Level, then Output, left to right in the same 196 px rack as every insert. Level and Output are pinned at the end of the rack: they are never removed or reordered.
  • Vertical meters with a real scale. dBFS ticks, colour zones (teal below −12, amber to 0, red above), a one-second peak-hold line, and a readout on each bar. Click a readout or a clip LED to reset it; the Reset peaks button goes.
  • The ceiling is drawn. A line across the Out bars at the ceiling, and a transfer curve that shows what the mode does. A dot rides the curve at the current input peak, so you see the stage working.
  • Level in dB. A fader reading +6.0 dB rather than a knob reading 2.00. The stored value stays the linear gain.
  • The stage lamp keeps today's latch rules (windsor#94 decision 3) and names what it latches: Limiting, Clipping, or Over 0 dB in Off.

No CPU when you aren't looking

  • The loop stops, not idles. Today each meter's frame loop keeps running every frame while the tab is hidden and checks closest('[hidden]') each time. The new meters start on the Mixer tab's show and stop on its hide, on visibilitychange, and when an IntersectionObserver says they are off screen.
  • Paint on a report, not on a frame. The stage reports 30 times a second; the meters paint only when a new report lands, whatever the display's refresh rate.
  • Compositor-only drawing. Each bar is a fixed gradient under a cover moved with transform, with a 34 ms transition to smooth between reports. That means no layout per frame, which <meter> value changes cause.
  • Text updates rarely. Readouts change only when the held value changes by 0.1 dB.
  • The transfer curve is static SVG. It redraws on a mode or ceiling edit; only the dot moves.
  • The top-bar light stays the always-on indicator. It reads the stage's watch and repaints only when its look changes. The stage's 30 Hz report keeps running for it and for the clip latch.
  • No CPU figures are claimed here. A worker measures before and after under docs/research/.

What tacowars chose

  1. Page order: B · Side column. The master column is sticky on the right; the master inserts and the sends scroll on the left.
  2. Meters: Segmented.
  3. Bridge: when the full meters scroll away.
  4. Master tap: dropped. The meters read the output stage's In and Out peaks, so they include the Music fader.
