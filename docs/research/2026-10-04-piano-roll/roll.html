Windsor Roll device

A new sequencer kind beside Grid and Figure: a piano roll over the selected region. Notes are absolute pitches with free start, length and velocity, on a loop that is the region's length or shorter. The keys on the left are coloured by how each pitch sits on the chord at the playhead (or under the pointer), and each note by the chord at its own start: a guide, never a limit. The Keys switch shows all twelve semitones or only the key's scale, and Fold shows only the rows that hold notes. It lives in the 244 px device, and Expand opens it wide.

It works: click empty space to add a note, drag a note to move it, drag its right edge to resize, click a note to delete it (right-click too), shift-drag to box-select, Delete removes the selection. Drag the velocity stems. Drag the brace's end on the ruler to change the loop. ▶ Play plays it with a plain test tone.

The device

  • The Figure's frame: 244 px, the rail with the region number, Split and Delete, plus Expand (amber). The tab bar has one page, Notes, and its summary gives the loop, the region, the note count and the key.
  • Two control columns: Snap (1/4 to 1/32, the triplets and Off), Loop in bars (− +, or the brace's end on the ruler), Keys (12 or Scale) and Fold; then Vel, the part's velocity, which scales every note's own as the Grid's Vel does, the two zooms, ↔ (Fit by default) and ↕, and Audition, which plays a note as you add it or drag it to a new pitch, or keeps quiet.
  • The roll: a bar ruler with the loop brace, a chord strip read from the Harmony tab, the keys down the left and a velocity lane under the notes. The body scrolls and the ruler, keys and lane follow it.
  • Past the loop the region's repeats are drawn hatched, with the loop's notes ghosted over the chords they will meet there (bars 5 to 8 here run Am F Dm E).
  • Expand opens the same device over the page with its own zoom, wide and tall enough to write in. Esc or the rail button closes it.

Colour, Keys and Fold

  • The keys are tinted by one chord: the one under the playhead while it plays, under the pointer while you edit, otherwise where the playhead stopped. The root is solid amber, the chord's other tones half amber, the key's scale tones a light grey, and keys outside the key stay plain. The corner names that chord and the chord strip lights it. Every row stays writable.
  • The selected note's key is outlined in ink with an edge against the roll, and shows its name even where the rows are too short for labels, so a note being dragged can be read off the keyboard. A selection lights every key it covers.
  • Notes take the tier of their own onset: amber on a chord tone, teal on a scale tone, grey with a red edge outside the key. A ghost repeat takes the chord it meets, so the loop's G♯ reads as out of key over G in bar 4 and as a chord tone over E in bar 8. Fill strength is velocity.
  • Keys: Scale keeps the key's seven rows and hides the other five. A row outside the scale that holds a note stays, as a thin grey sliver with the note greyed on it.
  • Fold shows only the rows that hold notes, in pitch order, and overrides Keys.

Draft data

  • A new kind, roll, appended to SEQUENCER_KINDS. It draws nothing, so it has no seed. Each region can carry its own roll, as every kind's pattern can (regionPattern).
  • No region comes with it. Setting a part to Roll puts nothing on the timeline, and clears the ∞ region a new part starts with; the author draws the first region, and the new roll's loop takes that region's length.
  • Vel is the part's existing velocity, the field the Grid's Vel knob sets; the roll adds no velocity of its own.
  • On the 24 PPQ grid: 1/32 is 3 ticks and 1/16T is 4, so Off snaps to 1 tick. A note held past the loop's end is cut there, and the region's end cuts it as it cuts every kind.
  • Notes overlap freely across pitches, up to the part's voices. A note dropped over another of the same pitch trims the earlier one.
sequencer: {
  kind: 'roll',
  loopTicks: 384,               // 4 bars, ≤ the region
  notes: [
    { tick: 0, ticks: 42, pitch: 57, velocity: 0.7 },
    …
  ]
}

Decided

  • The name is Roll, kind roll.
  • A click on a note deletes it, as in FL; a drag moves it and its right edge resizes it.
  • Note colours stay as drawn: amber chord tones, teal scale tones, grey with a red edge outside the key.
  • No automatic region: setting a part to Roll clears an ∞ region; the author draws the first one, and the roll takes its length. Drawn regions are kept, each roll taking its own region's length.
  • Shrinking the loop keeps the notes past it, hollow and silent.
  • A part Vel knob scales every note.
  • The keyboard is the colour guide, for the chord at the playhead or pointer; the rows stay plain.
  • Scale view hides the rows outside the scale.
  • The selected note's key is highlighted on the keyboard.
  • Audition is a switch on the device: on, a note sounds as you add it or drag it to a new pitch; off, editing is silent.
