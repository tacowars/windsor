The Euclid card from #356, fitted to the 244 px sequencer device the Grid, Chord, Arp and Basslead use. The pages, rows, cells and clicks are today's. What changes is the frame. The rail now holds the region number with Split and Delete, the controls sit in columns, and the hint, the readout line and the ? are gone. The device grows with its longest row, and the rack scrolls sideways when it outgrows the pane. The pattern is the one in your screenshot: E(5,16) with a Cutoff lane of 5 steps and Op A Level at 16.

▼ Part 1 — Euclid

What stays

  • The Pattern and Density tabs along the top, with the summary (k 5 · 3–9 · tri · 8 bars) at their right.
  • The rows, cell for cell: Ratchet, Trigger, then the lanes, at 22 px a step and grouped by four. Each lane keeps its − and + for its length, its ×, and its ↺ end mark.
  • The lane-view toggle on the rail: lanes at their own length, or laid out under this pass of the hits.
  • Every control on both pages, with today's labels and clicks.

What changes

  • 244 px high. The Ratchet and Trigger rows stay put. Three lanes fit, and a fourth scrolls under them. Lanes are 42 px, down from 44.
  • The rail gains the region number, Split and Delete, as on the other four devices. The pane loses its region row, and the rail drops the part name, which the pane header already shows.
  • No help. The ?, the "Hover a cell to read it" line and the hint paragraph go. Tooltips stay on the cells. A lane's hover reading sits under its name, as on the Grid.
  • Controls in columns: Note, Steps and Rotate; then Step and Capture; then Vel, Acc vel and Acc mod; then Hold.
  • + Lane moves into the Lanes rule, left of its dashed line. The "rows line up every…" note joins the section label.
  • Density keeps the device's width, and its plot takes the room left over. The modulator column is wide enough for LFOBARS, which today's card clips.
