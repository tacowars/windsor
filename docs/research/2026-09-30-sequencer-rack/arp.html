The device grows and shrinks with the arp's cycle, which follows the chord, Style and Octaves. Every control is today's.

From the Grid

  • The same device: 244 px, the rail with the region, Split and Delete, controls in columns, no hint paragraph.
  • The same strip: 32 px cells grouped by four, lanes stacked at 42 px, lane names and + Lane at the left, step rows held while the lanes scroll.
  • The same knob order: Octave over Octaves (the Grid's Length) and Rotate, then Vel, Acc vel and Acc mod, then Gate and Skip.

The arp's own

  • Style, Rate and Voicing in the first column, where the Grid has Step. Retrigger, Seed with Reseed, and Randomize in the second.
  • Its column is shorter: no degree picker, because the chord gives the pitch. So the step rows are 22 px shorter than the Grid's, and the lanes get that room.
  • A ratchet row NEW under S, as on the Grid and Basslead: click to cycle ×1 to ×4. A rest or a tie has no ratchet: its cell is grey and does nothing.
  • Its width moves with the cycle: 6 cells for Up over Am in two octaves, 30 for Up-down over Am7 in four. Cells past the cycle stay written but aren't shown, as today.
