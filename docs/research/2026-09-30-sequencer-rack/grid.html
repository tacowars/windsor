What stays

  • The step column, row for row: note cell, degree picker, Oct, A, S, then a cell per lane. The labels and interactions are today's.
  • Lanes stacked at 42 px (today's are 44), each named at the left with its × and its hover readout.
  • Split and Delete region, as icons on the rail under the region number.
  • + Lane in the corner above the lane names, where today's Lanes picker is.

What changes

  • Controls in columns instead of three rows above the strip. Step and Randomize are in the first column, then Octave, Length and Rotate, then the dynamics: Vel, Acc vel, Acc mod and Skip.
  • 32 px steps instead of 46. The device grows with the steps, any count from 1 to 32, so nothing is cut off. When it is wider than the pane, the rack scrolls sideways, as the insert rack does.
  • The step rows stay put. Two lanes fit at 244 px. A third or fourth scrolls under the step rows, with the lane names alongside.
  • No hint paragraph. Tooltips stay on the cells.
  • A ratchet row NEW under S, as Euclid's (#355): click to cycle ×1 to ×4. To keep two lanes in 244 px, the note and degree cells are 18 px and the lanes 42 px.

Agreed

  • Width follows the written steps, as the columns do today. Turning Length down greys the steps past it and keeps them, so the device keeps its width until those steps are gone.
