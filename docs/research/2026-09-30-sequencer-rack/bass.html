Windsor basslead device, round two

Basslead with a step strip, so a bass or lead part gets rhythm and variety. Each step can be a note, a tie or a rest, with Oct, accent, slide, a ratchet and the mod lanes, as on the Grid. The pitch still comes from the pitch mode, so the strip shapes when and how a note plays, not which note. Everything tagged NEW is a control Basslead doesn't have today. The values are your Reactor part's, except Rate, which is 1/8 here so the rhythm reads.

The strip

  • The Arp's top cell, ♪ — ·, because the pitch mode picks the note: Follow Root plays the chord's root, Follow Chord draws a chord tone, Fixed holds a degree.
  • A tie holds the note through the step, so a long note no longer depends on gate 1 and the same pitch twice.
  • Oct, A and S work as on the Grid: an octave jump, an accent (Acc vel and Acc mod), a slide into the next note.
  • Ratchet, as Euclid's (#355): click to cycle ×1, ×2, ×3, ×4, and the ticks show the split. A ratchet on a rest or a tie is drawn grey.
  • Lanes as on the Grid, stacked under step rows that stay put.

The controls

  • Pitch mode is a stack of three instead of a full-width bar, with Fixed degree under it, greyed unless Fixed is on.
  • Rate, Seed with the Reseed icon, and Randomize. Randomize rerolls the rhythm: notes, ties, rests, octaves, accents, slides and ratchets.
  • The knobs follow the Grid's order: Octave, Length and Rotate, then Vel, Acc vel and Acc mod, then Gate, Density and Root bias. Root bias is greyed unless Follow Chord is on.
  • Density stays: the chance a note step sounds. At 1.00 every note step plays.

Agreed

  • Agreed: the Grid and the Arp get the same ratchet row, and Basslead gets no pitch lane.
  • The octave modifier is the per-step Oct row, under the note cell.
  • Old songs. A Basslead with no strip would load as a strip of plain notes, one bar long, which plays exactly as today. Nothing needs a version bump.
