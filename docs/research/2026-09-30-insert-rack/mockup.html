Windsor insert rack

Mockups of two ideas. Every insert is the same height and only its width varies, the way Bitwig and Live lay out their device chains. The send buses become racks of the same inserts, so the plate and the echo can be swapped out or joined by others. Switch the layout and height in the bar below. Every control is a real control of today's insert, except Mix on the plate and the echo, which is new.

Pages

  • Every insert fits one height. A complex insert splits into tabs in its title bar, like Live's Echo (Echo, Modulation, Character).
  • Chains stay short. Advanced Drive is about as wide as its widest page, not all of its pages together.
  • The cost: only one page of an insert shows at a time, so a stage's filter and its modulation amounts are never on screen together.

All sections, wide

  • Every control stays visible, as in Bitwig. The sections sit side by side with a small label above each.
  • The cost: Advanced Drive alone is over 1,000 px wide, so a chain of two or three complex inserts scrolls sideways. Folding an insert to its name helps.
  • A mix of the two is possible: wide by default, with Advanced Drive and Tape using pages.

Open questions

  • Bus names. Room and Echo stop describing a bus once its effect can be swapped. The mockup says Send A and Send B. They could also take names the user types.
  • How many buses? Two, as today, or an Add bus button?
  • Mix on the plate and the echo. 1.00 on a bus, as today. On a part, the default would be lower (0.30 in the mockup).
  • Advanced Drive stages. Serial and parallel routings have two stages, and each stage gets its own page.
  • Tape is being reworked in another session (windsor#146). Its controls here are today's.
