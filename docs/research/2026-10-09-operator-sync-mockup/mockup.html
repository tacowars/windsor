Operator Sync and LFO Ratio

Today's Parts tab with Digital Bass loaded, plus a Sync picker on every operator row and Ratio A–D on both LFO cards (dashed outlines mark them; the toggle above hides the outlines). Every row's picker lists Off, Note and the other three operators; a letter that would close a loop is struck through, with the reason under it. Try B → A, then open A's picker. C starts synced to the note so the at-a-glance face shows from the start; All Sync off clears it. The Ratio knobs drag (shift for fine, double-click resets) so the signed readout can be seen.

To decide

  • Where the picker sits. Beside the ratio pair: a cell in the knob row after Coarse, Fine and the Ratio readout, reading as part of the pitch controls; the main group grows by one cell. Beside the wave picker: a button on the wave line, reading as part of what the operator is; the identity column grows from 138 px to 170 px to fit it.

The proposal for the glance

  • A synced operator's picker face shows its master: the letter in that operator's colour, or ♪ for the note, on a tint of the same colour. Off reads as a dim dash (or Sync on the wave line).
  • The LFO Ratio readout is signed octaves, −4 to +4, written +1.25 as Width A–D are; the unit is in the knob's hint, because +1.25oct overruns the cell.

Stand-ins

  • The left column (Output, Algorithm, Global) is a labelled box: it does not change.
  • Only the Sync picker, its menu and the Ratio knobs respond. The captured controls are pictures of today's, at Digital Bass's real values; the Ratio knobs start at 0, the field's default.
  • The envelope plots are pictures taken at a 1440 px window. At other widths they stretch, where the console redraws them.
