Windsor Figure device

The Figure (windsor#483) in the agreed 244 px sequencer device: the Arp's rail and Play columns, the Grid's 32 px strip with a tone cell and a Vel row, and a Process page behind page tabs as the Euclid device has. The cells are a written line over the current chord; the tone labels follow the chord under the playhead. Three presets below stand for the three processes: Glass's additive figure, Reich's Clapping Music drift, and a canon on another part.

From the Arp and the Grid

  • The same device: 244 px, the rail with the region, Split and Delete, controls in columns, no hint paragraph.
  • The Arp's Play columns: Rate, Seed with Reseed and Randomize; Octave, Length and Rotate; Vel, Acc vel and Acc mod; Gate and Skip. Length and Rotate are the Grid's.
  • The Grid's strip: 32 px cells grouped by four, step rows held while the lanes scroll, + Lane in the corner, a ratchet row under S. The page tabs take 20 px the Arp never had, so the Cells page has no section labels: the tab bar names the page and its summary names the chord, the stage, the drift and the source. The head rows are 1 px apart, and two lanes still fit.

The Figure's own

  • The tone cell NEW is a chord tone, not a note: a click cycles rest → tie → note, and a picker chooses −8..8. Its label comes from the chord under the playhead through figureNote, so the same cells read R 3 5 over Am and R 4 5 over Fsus4. A prime is an octave up (R'), a minus an octave down (−5).
  • A Vel row NEW per cell, 0..1, scaling the part's Vel. A cell at 1 reads faint, so the written accents stand out.
  • Page tabs: Cells, where everything plays, and Process, where the three processes live. The summary at the tabs' right names the stage, the drift and the source in play.
  • The playhead lights the cell that sounds after the schedule and the drift. Cells past the stage's length are dimmed, not hidden, so the full line stays in view while a short stage plays.

Process page

  • Schedule is a row of stage chips, length × bars: drag to reorder, × to remove, + to add. The stage in play is outlined. The readout gives the full cycle.
  • Drift is two knobs, Steps −4..4 and Every N bars, with a readout: "+1 step / 12 bars".
  • Source is a part picker of the song's Figure parts, "Own cells" first, with Offset and Transpose. With a source set, the Cells strip draws the leader's cells greyed and read-only, with the follower's own playhead on them (the Canon preset).

Open questions

  • Vel: a bar cell you drag (the Vel toggle above, "Bar cell"), or a small dial ("Dial")? The bar reads at a glance and matches the lanes; the dial matches the knobs.
  • Tone labels: chord degrees, R 3 5 7 (the stack's intervals, so a sus chord reads R 4 5), or stack positions, 1 2 3 4 (always in order, whatever the chord)?
