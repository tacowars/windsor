# The ensemble insert and the Juno chorus presets: sources (#695)

What each preset's numbers rest on, which are stated by a source and which are
approximations, and the machines dropped for want of a source (#695
decision 9: a machine that cannot be sourced is dropped, never invented). The
numbers themselves live in `packages/client/src/audio/inserts/`
(`chorusPresetTables.ts`, `ensembleConstants.ts`, `ensemblePresetTables.ts`);
this note is their provenance. Web research on 2026-09-25; no paid source.

**The one gap every string machine shares.** No public source found gives a
BBD delay swing in ms, or the BBD clock (and so the line centre), for any
string machine. Only the Juno-60's delays are measured. So every ensemble
preset's depths and centre are approximations, scaled from the Juno's
measured ±1.85 ms slow swing and the Logan's stated slow : fast mix.

## Juno-60 chorus → the chorus presets (measured)

Source: pendragon-andyh, *Juno60 / Chorus*,
<https://github.com/pendragon-andyh/Juno60/blob/master/Chorus/README.md> —
measurements from recordings (Sonic Visualiser).

| Mode | LFO | Delay sweep | Stereo | Preset (`rate`, `depth`, `spread`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | 0.513 Hz triangle | 1.66–5.35 ms | stereo, right line's modulation inverted | 0.513 Hz, 1.845 ms, 1 |
| II | 0.863 Hz | 1.66–5.35 ms | stereo | 0.863 Hz, 1.845 ms, 1 |
| I + II | 9.75 Hz | 3.3–3.7 ms | mono ("better described as a vibrato") | 9.75 Hz, 0.2 ms, 0 |

`depth` is half the sweep. The service notes quoted there give 0.5, 0.83 and
"1" Hz (the author reads the last as 10 Hz); the measured rates are used. What
the chorus insert does not model: its own line centres (11 and 17 ms, not the
Juno's ≈ 3.5 ms), its second voice at 1.37 × the rate, a sine LFO rather than
a triangle, and the Juno's 12 dB pre-BBD lowpass (cutoff not given). So these
are the Juno's rates and swings on this chorus, not the Juno circuit.

## ARP / Eminent Solina and Logan String Melody → `solina` (approximation)

- **Structure (stated).** J. Haible, *Triple Chorus*,
  <http://jhaible.com/legacy/triple_chorus/triple_chorus.html>: three BBD
  lines; two three-phase LFOs, slow ("Chorus") and fast ("Vibrato"), roughly
  120° apart; each line's clock swept by the sum of both at the same phase; a
  second-order **12 kHz** filter before the lines; TCA350 BBDs. The LFOs are
  filtered squares, so only approximately sine, and Haible notes the three
  phases cannot be perfectly balanced for both LFOs at once.
- **Rates (stated).** Logan String Melody teardown by an owner, T. Kopper,
  <https://till-kopper.de/logan_string_melody.html> (cited by Wikipedia):
  slow "about 0.6 Hz", fast 6.0 Hz, phases 0°, 120°, 240°, mixed **79 % slow
  to 21 % fast**, three TCA350s. The same 0.6 / 6 Hz for the Solina from a
  synth-DIY thread (<https://www.modwiggler.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=265377&start=50>,
  read through web.archive.org; an anonymous poster, so low authority), which
  also says "quite shallow depth" and "there's no dry signal mixed in".
- **Output.** Mono on the original (Synthoma Élkorus guide,
  <https://www.analoguehaven.com/synthoma/elkorusv3/userguide.pdf>); the insert's
  default Width is decision 8's 1, and Width 0 is the original.
- **Preset.** slow 0.6 Hz / 1.2 ms, fast 6 Hz / 0.32 ms, centre 5 ms, tone
  12 kHz. Stated: the rates and the tone. Approximated: the slow depth (below
  the Juno's ±1.85 ms, for "quite shallow"), the fast depth (the slow × 21/79,
  the Logan's CV mix taken as a delay-swing ratio) and the centre (no clock).
  These are the kind's defaults, and the default Mix is 1 (no dry signal).
- **One preset for two machines.** Every value the sources give for the Logan
  equals the Solina's, and the chips are the same, so two presets would be
  indistinguishable and the picker could not tell them apart: the preset is
  labelled "ARP Solina / Logan String Melody".

## Roland RS-202 → `rs-202` (approximation)

- **Rates (stated).** RS-202 service notes (1976),
  <https://archive.org/details/Roland_RS-202_Service_Notes>, p. 7, the
  modulator's scope traces: fast "160 msec (6.25 Hz)" (ensemble switch at I),
  slow "(0.66 Hz)". Not the keyboard vibrato, a separate circuit at 6.8 Hz.
- **Structure (stated).** Three MN3002 (512-stage) lines — the schematic and
  Sound On Sound, <https://www.soundonsound.com/reviews/roland-rs202-multivox-mx202-retrozone>,
  which calls mode I "the faster, deeper ensemble".
- **Preset.** slow 0.66 Hz / 1.5 ms, fast 6.25 Hz / 0.4 ms, centre 8 ms,
  tone 12 kHz. Stated: the rates. Approximated: the depths (deeper than the
  Solina's, per SOS; fast = slow × 21/79), the centre (a 512-stage line
  against the TCA350's 185 stages; the clock is not labelled on the trace)
  and the tone (the Solina's stated filter; none is given for the RS-202).
  Mode II's rates are not given, so there is no mode II preset.

## Dropped

- **Roland RS-505.** The service manual states four SAD512D lines and a 40–250
  kHz clock (so roughly 1.0–6.4 ms), and four modulating waves, but no
  ensemble LFO rate (the "1–15 Hz" in the manual is the synth LFO). No rate,
  no preset.
- **Elka Rhapsody 610.** A two-line chorus with its modulations 180° apart
  (Élkorus guide; T. Kopper, <https://till-kopper.de/elka.html>) — not the
  three-phase design this kind models — and no stated rate: the guide's
  4 Hz / 0.6 Hz is a suggested setting for its own clone.
- **Crumar Performer / Multiman-S.** Solina-type by structure (Haible;
  <https://till-kopper.de/crumar_performer.html>; service manual boards
  "P 550 – LFO for analog delay lines"), but no source states a rate, depth or
  delay.

## Bounds

Every sourced value sits inside decision 7's bounds (slow 0.513–0.66 Hz; fast
6–9.75 Hz; the Juno's 1.66–5.35 ms sweep; the RS-505's ≈ 1–6.4 ms; the
Solina's 12 kHz filter), so none was widened.
