MIDI CC mapping

Today's Parts tab: the part bar around the sequencer picker and operator A's row of knobs, captured from the dev build with a song of one part, Acid (Acid Saw on a Grid, the macros Accent, Squelch and Grit, a Dub delay insert, and lanes on the delay's Feedback and on LFO 1 Amt). New controls have dashed outlines, which the Additions toggle hides. The decisions behind them are in docs/log/2026-10-13-midi-cc-mapping.md. The toolbar steps through every state. Panel opens the mapping panel, Add walks through adding a mapping, Level knob shows operator A's Level mapped to CC 71, and Right-click opens the knob menu. Reset performance in the panel works.

The proposal

  • MIDI CC is a part-bar button after the sequencer picker. Its badge counts the selected part's mappings (6 for Acid). It opens the panel as a popover under the button.
  • The panel has one row per mapping. The columns are Part, Parameter (with the lane picker's kind line), Source (device and channel, each Any by default and narrowable, the CC, and what learn heard), Range (min and max in the target's units, which you drag like a knob or double-click to type), Inv, Live (the CC's value, or a status: Waiting for pickup, Automated, Inert), Learn (remap the source) and Delete. The header holds This part | All parts, Pickup | Jump and Reset performance. The foot holds + Add mapping and the part's voice count against 8.
  • + Add mapping opens the lane picker's target list. It is grouped Mixer, Inserts, Voice and Macros, shows Voice n/8, leaves out what a macro maps, the insert's On switch and the sequencer rows, and tags targets already mapped or automated. It then goes straight into learn on a new row: "Move a knob on your controller…" with Cancel, then "Heard CC 74 · ch 1 · USB MIDI Controller" as it commits. The new row's range is the target's full range.
  • The knob. A mapped knob shows CC 71 in the tag slot under its value, where AUTO, ENV and a macro's name sit today. When the CC moves it, the pin stays on the patch's 0.80, and the env ring's arc and dot run to the live 0.30. Waiting for pickup, a hollow marker sits at the controller's position (0.21), and the tag is outlined. While learning, the knob is lit and a hint sits under it.
  • Right-click on a knob whose target is in the catalog gives "Learn MIDI CC…". On a mapped knob it also gives "Edit mapping…" and "Remove CC 71". Coarse, Fine, Detune, Vel, LP, HP, Key Trk, Attack, Sustain and Release are not catalog targets, so they keep the browser's menu.
  • MIDI not enabled: the panel still lists the mappings, dimmed, with a banner and the keyboard bar's Enable MIDI button. Learn and + Add mapping wait for it.

LFO 1 Amt has a song lane that is on, so its row reads Automated and the CC is ignored. Acid's voice slots are LFO 1 Amt (its lane, which the CC shares), Op A Level, Cutoff, Squelch and Op B Decay: Voice 5/8. Send A is a strip target and takes none.

To decide

  • A popover or a docked pane? Recommended: a popover under the MIDI CC button, as drawn. Mapping is setup you open, change and close. A docked pane would take height from the operator rows on every visit, and the Song tab's shape panel went the same way (2026-10-01-song-automation-lanes decision 13). "Edit mapping…" from a knob on another tab opens the same popover over that tab.
  • Where the CC tag sits. Recommended: in the tag slot under the value, as drawn, where AUTO, ENV and a macro's name sit. Every knob size has that slot, including the rack's and the Song tab's compact knobs (whose bubble carries the tag). A lane's AUTO wins it, since a lane locks the knob and the CC is ignored, and a CC wins over ENV. The other choice, a corner badge on the dial, would crowd the 38 px dial and the ring that CC and ENV draw.

Stand-ins

  • The rail (Output, Algorithm, Global) and the rows under operator A are not drawn. The rail keeps its width, so the operator row wraps as it does in the build.
  • Operator A's envelope picture is redrawn by hand from the capture. In the build it is a canvas.
  • The parts Pad and Kick in All parts, the device names USB MIDI Controller and Second Controller, and every CC number are made up for the mockup.
  • Op A Level's Live cell follows the Level knob toggle: at rest it has heard no value yet, moved it reads CC 89 (0.30), and waiting it reads CC 100 (0.21) against the patch's 0.80.
  • Only the toolbar, the panel's two segments and Reset performance respond. Everything else is a picture.
