Euclid lanes

The Euclid part as a rack device: a ratchet row above the trigger row and drawn lanes below it. Each lane has its own length and is read when a hit sounds, so accents, pitch and tone fall against the hits in polymeter, while the density modulator still moves the hits on bar lines. Press Play to hear a stand-in tom; every cell can be edited.

What to judge

  1. Pages. The device has two pages, as an insert with more than one does: Pattern (the Play knobs, the ratchet and trigger rows and the lanes) and Density (the modulator, its plot and the k bounds). The tab row keeps a one-line readout of the modulator, so you can see k move without leaving Pattern.
  2. The lane-view toggle sits on the rail, between the name and the ?. The top button shows each lane at its own length; the bottom one lays each lane out under this bar's hits, with a notch where a lane starts over and the cells under a rest dimmed.
  3. The ratchet row sits directly over the trigger row, one thin cell per step. A click cycles ×1, ×2, ×3, ×4; the ticks show the subdivision. A ratchet over a rest is drawn grey: it waits until a hit lands there.
  4. The density modulator is unchanged: it moves k between k min and k max on each bar line. Watch the trigger row re-cut while the ratchet and lane rows stay put, so ratchets come and go as hits land on them.
  5. The full cycle in the toolbar: how many bars until the trigger's steps and every lane line up again.
  6. Height. With density on its own page, the Pattern page is the Play knobs plus the rows. Each lane still adds a row (16 px for accent, 44 px for pitch or a sound lane), so a full stack of accent, pitch and four sound lanes is about 330 px of rows.

How to edit

  • Trigger: click a cell to flip it. The figure freezes and the row shows Release, which lets the modulator back in.
  • Accent: click to toggle.
  • Pitch: drag up or down on a cell, one semitone per 6 px, ±24. Double-click resets to 0.
  • Sound lanes: press or drag across cells to paint, as the grid's lanes do. Double-click resets to 0.
  • Lane length is the − + in each lane's name column, beside its remove ×. Add lane offers Accent and Pitch once each and up to four sound lanes.

Stand-in sound

  • The preview's tom follows Accent, Pitch, Cutoff, Resonance, the decays and operator level. The real part plays its FM patch.
