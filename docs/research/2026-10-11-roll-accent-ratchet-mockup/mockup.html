Accents and ratchets on Roll notes

Today's Roll device holding the Acid Saw bar after a Bounce to Roll: one bar of 12 notes, looping across a 2-bar region (the second bar is the loop's repeat, drawn dim behind the hatch). The proposal gives every Roll note its own accent and ratchet, as a Grid step has, and edits them in the lane under the notes, where velocity is today. Dashed outlines mark what is new; the toolbar hides them. Click the cells in the lane: Acc toggles a note's accent, Rat steps its ratchet.

Reading the roll

chord tone (A, C, E under A min) in the key, not the chord (G, D) outside the key accented: a white top edge (new) ratchet ×3: split into its hits (new)
The amber and teal are today's harmony colours, not accent or slide: they say how each pitch sits in the chord. A brighter fill is a louder note.

The proposal

  1. Per note. A Roll note gains accent (on or off) and ratchet (×1 to ×4, the Grid's RATCHET_MAX). Absent means a plain note, so every song saved today loads and plays as it does now.
  2. The lane switch. The corner under the keys that reads VEL today becomes three stacked tabs, Vel · Acc · Rat, and picks what the lane shows. Vel is today's stems, unchanged. The choice is the view's, never written to the song, as Notes · Envelopes is.
  3. Acc. One square under each note's onset, the Grid's A cell: lit in the note's colour when accented. A click toggles it.
  4. Rat. One cell under each note, as wide as the note, holding its hits: its count, then one dim bar at ×1 or two to four lit bars, one per hit. A click steps ×1 → ×2 → ×3 → ×4 → ×1; shift-click steps back.
  5. Marks on the notes. Whatever the lane shows, an accented note has a white top edge and a ratcheted note is split into its hits, so the roll reads both without a switch.
  6. The Velocity row. Under both control columns, one row holds Vel (moved down from the top of the second column) beside the new Acc vel and Acc mod: the Grid's trio, one per roll. The device grows 40 px taller (244 to 284 px) and no wider, and the roll gets the extra rows.
  7. Accent's sound. An accented note plays its velocity × Vel + Acc vel, at most 1, and adds Acc mod to the wheel for that note alone (the Grid's per-note mod, #602). The note's own velocity is left as drawn.
  8. Ratchet's sound. A ratchet of N plays N hits spaced evenly in time across the note's length, each held until the next, through the engine's own ratchet roll (rollHits) with the note's length as the span. The hits are exact: nothing is rounded to the 24 PPQ grid.
  9. Selection. With notes selected, a click on one of their cells sets all of them to the clicked cell's new state. One click is one undo step.

What it changes elsewhere

  • Bounce to Roll (shipped: #744, #745, #747) today raises the part's Vel to fit its accents, drops their mod, and writes a ratchet as separate notes rounded to 24 PPQ, counting the losses in its toast. With this, it writes the accent as the flag and copies the part's Acc vel and Acc mod onto the roll, and writes a ratcheted step as one note ×N. The toast's "accents lost their mod", "ratchet hits lost" and the ratchets' "ticks rounded" go, and a 1/32 ×4 plays exactly. A new record supersedes those parts of 2026-10-11-bounce-to-roll.
  • Format. Additive: two optional note fields, and the roll's Acc vel and Acc mod default to the Grid's. ARRANGEMENT_VERSION is not bumped.
  • windsor#735, mod-wheel and pitch-bend lanes, is dropped: accents and ratchets were its demand.

Decided (tacowars, 2026-10-12)

  • Monophonic only. Notes that start together (a chord) take no accent or ratchet: the lane draws no cell for them, and the normaliser clears both from a note that comes to share its onset.
  • Shortest ratchet. A note shorter than a 1/32 (3 ticks) takes no ratchet; its Rat cell is disabled.
  • Recording. A hard-played note records its velocity, never an accent.
The decisions are recorded in docs/log/2026-10-12-roll-note-accent-and-ratchet.md.
