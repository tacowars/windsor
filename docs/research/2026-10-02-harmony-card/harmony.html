What changes

  • Accidental (♭ ♮ ♯) moves the whole chord a semitone. ♮ is today's chord.
  • Quality is Scale's own (today) or one of the twelve named qualities. Picking one sets Size to match; pressing Triad or Seventh goes back to the scale's own chord.
  • The degree chips still show the scale's own chords. The pressed chip stays the degree when ♭/♯ or a quality is set.
  • Delete moves to the bottom row's right end, away from the info bubble.

Play buttons and the info bubblenew

  • Hold a ▶ to hear its degree's chord; it stops on release. It doesn't select the degree or change the song, so you can press along the row to try a progression.
  • Which chord: the selected degree's ▶ plays the block as written, ♭/♯ and quality included. The others play the scale's own chord at the current Size.
  • The info bubble names the selected block's chord (G# maj, ♭VI · triad). While you hold a ▶ it names the chord sounding instead.
  • The voice is one fixed soft FM pad from the library on the unused audition aux strip. It skips the song master and the song's mixer, so mute, solo and the faders never touch it.
  • The voicing is close, around middle C, with the root an octave below, so the quality is easy to hear whatever the song's parts do.
  • It plays with the transport stopped or running.
