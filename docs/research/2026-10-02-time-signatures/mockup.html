Decided tacowars, 2026-10-02: each as drawn and recommended here

1 · The picker

A plain select in the swing group, where 4/4 sits today, with the six meters in the engine's order. It's one step on undo. Options read the bare meter, so 7/8 doesn't say 2+2+3 until a grouping can be chosen.

Decided: as drawn.

2 · Bar line heavier?

Today a bar is a full-height line and a beat is a 6px tick, so the ruler already tells them apart. In a step strip a bar shows only when the part runs past one bar (a 16-step pattern kept in 7/8 groups 4·4·6 | 2). Try it with the Bar line switch.

Decided: ruler as today; in step strips, a wider gap (10px) before a new bar. The toolbar's Strip + ruler also draws the ruler's bar lines heavier, for comparison.

3 · 7/8's long beat

The third beat is just a longer group (6 cells at 1/16, 3 at 1/8), with no tint or label. The ruler's ticks fall at 24 and 48, so the last gap is visibly longer.

Decided: as drawn, no extra mark.

4 · Grouping at other step lengths

Today the Grid strip groups every 4 steps at any step length, and Euclid already groups by the beat. The beat rule changes 4/4 at 1/8 to 2·2·2·2 (today 4·4) and at 1/32 to groups of 8.

Decided: group by beat when a beat holds 2+ steps; otherwise a gap at each bar; when a step is a bar or longer, groups of 4 as today. Grid and Euclid share the rule.

5 · Counting the /8 meters

The third field counts 16ths inside the counted beat (1–6 in a dotted-quarter beat), so 6/8 tick 36 reads 1.2.1 and 7/8's last 16th of bar 2 reads 2.3.6. The other option counts 8ths in /8 meters (1–3), which loses the 16th.

Decided: 16ths, as the issue proposes. Both rows are drawn above.

6 · Step picker's 96

Reads 1 bar in 4/4 and 1/1 in every other meter. Open the card's Step select to see it.

Decided: as the issue decides. The Euclid card's cells take the same beat rule and aren't drawn here.
