bloom · console card

The rack card for bloom, the harmonic pad generator insert (epic windsor#710). All 15 controls sit on one page, grouped by function: Envelope, Generator, Bands, Dynamics and Output, with today's rules between the groups. The rail is any insert's rail. The rack stays one height (196 px), and only the width grows.

Every value is from the prepared port's bloomConstants.ts: ranges, defaults and steps. The knobs drag (double-click resets), so the readouts can be tried across their ranges. The pickers, Freeze, the switches, the slot tab and the folds work. Nothing makes sound.

The groups, left to right

  • Envelope: Swell and Sustain, then Freeze under them. Freeze is the held case of Sustain, so it sits in their column.
  • Generator: Shape and Bright, then Octave, then the Key and Scale pickers in a wide column. Key and Scale are lists and are not automation targets, so they're pickers, as every enum on a card is today.
  • Bands: Low and High, which step through the 48 bands by note, then Focus.
  • Dynamics: Density, alone.
  • Output: Amount and Spread, then Mix at the big dial, last, as every insert's Mix is.

Freeze, and the slot

  • Freeze is a latching button with a lamp. Off, it is an outlined button. On, it fills in the chain's accent (teal on a strip, amber on a send bus). The rail's ⏻ stays the insert's power, a glyph with no box. While a lane holds Freeze, it takes the locked ring in the lane's colour, as the rail's switch does (windsor#631).
  • The slot (E2) is today's slot area as Echo has it: the collapsed › tab at the right edge, the picker after a rule following Mix, and the child inline with its own rail. bloom's card draws none of it. E1 ships the card as row 1 shows it.

Questions for tacowars
