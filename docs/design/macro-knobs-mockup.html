What this shows

The patch has two macros. Accent is selected: it maps Op A Level, Cutoff and Op A Decay, which is the 303's accent in one knob. Wobble is held by a song lane (the AUTO ring). In the Filter card above, Cutoff is locked under Accent's name: turning it would do nothing audible while the mapping exists, and the dial shows the value Accent plays at its current position. Every mapping row reads what it plays now in amber. The Macros card is the deck's last card and takes the full width, so a mapping row has room for its two knobs, its curve and its readout.

A keeps the macros as tiles with one list of mappings for the selected one; it reads like the LFO cards. B gives every macro a row with its mappings as chips, so all mappings show at once and a chip opens a small popover to edit. A is the recommendation: the min and max knobs stay visible, and a 303 accent is edited by ear, knob under hand.
