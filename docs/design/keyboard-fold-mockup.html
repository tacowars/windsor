What changes

  1. Fold: Off · Scale · Chord, one setting per part, saved in the song (MusicPart.fold, default Off). The keyboard plays the selected part, so the three copies of the control all show and edit that part's field: on the Parts tab's keys row after Oct +, in the expanded keyboard's bar after Rows, and on the Roll device under Rec. A drum part stays Off while a bass part stays folded.
  2. Folded keys are one equal rectangle per tone of the set, with no black keys, each named (A, C, E). The first key is the set's root in the octave the label names (A3), or, when a custom scale leaves the root out, the set's first tone above it (root C with [2, 5, 9] starts on D3, and the label says D3). The row ends on the first key's note an octave or two up: (octaves × tones) + 1 keys. Scale is the song's key and scale; Chord is the chord at the playhead, the scale when the song has no chords. Step the chord above, or press Run: the keys rename at each chord.
  3. Held notes. Tap keys to light them as held notes, then step the chord. A held note keeps sounding when the keys rename; its key stays lit only where a shown key still carries that note. The finger's next move onto another key plays that key.
  4. Computer keys (tick Computer keys above): while folded, the home row a s d f g h j k l ; ' plays the first eleven keys, and w e t y u o p play nothing. z and x still shift the octave. With fewer than eleven keys shown, the letters past the row play the set's next tones, unlit.
  5. MIDI notes outside the set are ignored: not sounded, not recorded, not lit. A note a little early for a chord change is kept when it belongs to the chord coming within 1/16 (6 ticks). The Roll's recorder is unchanged; the fold is its filter.
  6. The Roll device is drawn as the Roll zoom spec lays it out (windsor#910, option B: Show Loop | Region, zoom bars, Dup), not as main does today. The metronome moves beside Rec to make room for the fold under it.

MIDI's grace window, at 120 BPM

A tick is 500 ms ÷ 24 = 20.8 ms, so the proposed window of 1/16 (6 ticks) is 125 ms. A min plays bar 1 and F maj starts at bar 2, tick 96. The recorder stamps whole ticks, the tick sounding when the note is heard. An F heard 15 ms before the change is stamped tick 95: A min (A C E) does not hold F, but the window's ticks are 96 to 101, and F maj starts at 96 and holds it, so the F is kept, sounded and recorded. A B heard at the same moment is in neither chord and is dropped. An F 150 ms early (stamped tick 88) is dropped too: the window's ticks are 89 to 94, before the change.

To decide

  1. How a root key is marked. Recommended: its name carries the octave (A3, A4, A5) in full ink, and a 2 px rule along its top. The other keys show the letter only.
  2. The colors. Recommended: Scale keeps today's key color, and the tones of the chord at the playhead carry a 3 px amber strip at the foot, the Roll's chord tier, so the chord shows inside the scale and moves with Run. Chord needs no strip, since every key is a chord tone. A held key keeps today's amber fill.
  3. Where Fold sits. Recommended: after Oct + on the Parts row (it costs the desktop's keys 176 px, a white key 41.8 to 33.1 px); after Rows in the expanded bar (in portrait the bar wraps to two lines and the keys lose 36 px of height); on the Roll device as the last item of the second column, labelled Keyboard fold, with the metronome moved beside Rec.
  4. Whether the closing root key stays, and in two rows whether every row ends on its root (the lower row's last key and the upper row's first are the same note, and both light), only the top row does (the lower rows keep an empty slot so the columns line up), or none does. Recommended: every row.
  5. The Roll device's label. The Roll already has a Fold (All | Fold) that hides its empty rows. Recommended: Keyboard fold for the new one; the switcher above shows Fold.
  6. The landscape row with a seven-tone Scale. Recommended: let Enable MIDI wrap (the row grows 42 px while such a fold is on). The other way is a Fold control without its label on the Parts row (136 px), which leaves the keys 680.3 px, 45.4 px a key, on one line.
  7. The grace window. Proposed 1/16, 6 ticks, 125 ms at 120 BPM. The hand decides.
  8. The chord fold's register. The first key is the chord's root in the label's octave, so under A min the keys start at A3 (45) and under C maj at C3 (36): the row moves by up to eleven semitones as the chord changes. Proposed as drawn; the other way starts each chord at its root nearest the key's root.
