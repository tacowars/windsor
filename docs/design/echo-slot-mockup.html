Echo · wet-path slot

Echo's rack card with a slot: one child insert on Echo's wet path, before Echo's Mix (epic windsor#686). The card is drawn as variant A of the bloom mockup. The slot picker sits rightmost in Echo's panel, after Echo's own controls. The child sits inline after the picker, with a small rail of its own. Clicking the child's name folds it to that rail. The rack stays one height (196 px), and only the width grows.

Echo's own cells are today's, cell for cell: Sync to 120 bpm, Time, Regen, Damp, Q and Mix, and the rail with its switch, name and ◀ ▶ ✕. The children's cells are today's cards too, Mix included. Read the Today row first for comparison. The slot pickers, switches, page tabs and folds work in every row, so any state can be reached from any other. The knobs are drawings at today's values and don't turn. Nothing makes sound.

How it works

  • The slot column follows a rule after Echo's Mix. It holds a picker labelled Slot and a short note. The picker lists None first, then the Add picker's groups minus Echo (insertAddPicker.ts, INSERT_GROUPS).
  • The child's rail holds its own on/off switch and its name, SLOT · <KIND>, in the rack's accent, and nothing else. It has no ◀ ▶ ✕: the child can't move or leave the slot. Setting the picker back to None removes it.
  • The child's page is its card as it is on the rack today, its own Mix included (epic decision 3). A child with pages shows its own tabs over the child's area only.

Folding

  • Clicking the child's name folds it to its rail (21 px with its border). Echo goes back to its own width, and the picker still names the child. Clicking the name again opens it.
  • Clicking Echo's own name folds the whole insert, child included, to the rack's 26 px fold, as any insert folds today.
  • Switching Echo off dims its child with it, since Echo bypasses its whole wet path. Switching the child off dims only the child.
