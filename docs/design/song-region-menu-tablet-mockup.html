The touch rules

  • Opening: pointer-timed, because iOS Safari fires no contextmenu on a long press. A touch or pen press on a lane (longPressOpens) starts a 450 ms timer (LONG_PRESS); at 450 ms within 8 px (longPressDue) it selects the region, or for a gap the part, as the desktop right-click does, and opens the menu. Moving past the slop cancels the timer and the press carries on as today's drag. The release that ends an opening press does nothing: no tap select, no bar added on a gap (the opened flag wireChipColour already keeps for the chip). pointercancel cancels.
  • No callout, no magnifier: .lane already has touch-action: none and user-select: none, and the Song tab's panel -webkit-user-select: none (windsor#814), so iOS starts no text selection or loupe. Nothing in console.css sets -webkit-touch-callout; the build adds -webkit-touch-callout: none to .lane so no system callout can appear, and the lane's contextmenu handler calls preventDefault (it is what a trackpad's two-finger click on the tablet fires, and opens the desktop menu).
  • Size: 44 px rows (the common 44 pt touch-target minimum; on this screen 1 CSS px is 1 pt), labels 15 px, the header line 10.5 px, tags 11 px, at least 260 px wide, 16 px side padding. The Grid region menu is then about 640 px tall, which fits either orientation without scrolling. A screen too short for a menu caps it and it scrolls inside itself, as .song-menu does.
  • Placement: beside the finger, never under the hand. The menu's right edge sits 32 px left of the touch point (the fingertip's radius plus a gap) and its top 4 px under the pressed lane, so the menu is below-left of the finger, clear of a right hand, and the pressed lane, the lanes above it and the ruler's loop strip stay in view for the preview. With no room on the left (under 32 + width + 8 px) it flips to the right of the finger. It is then clamped 8 px inside the screen on both axes, over the header and pane if it must; a menu too tall for the room under its lane slides up only as far as the bottom edge needs (the Arp state in landscape overlaps arp's lane by 26 px, left of the finger). There is no handedness setting: the menu always tries the finger's left first. The menu is fixed over the page, as .knob-cc-menu is.
  • Choosing, and the preview: with the finger still down, sliding onto a row lights it and shows the desktop's hover preview (dashed box, loop brace, split line); lifting on that row runs it, lifting off every row runs nothing. A lift without sliding leaves the menu open, and a tap on a row runs it without a preview. A disabled row lights but runs nothing.
  • Set sequencer ▸ drills in: a tap swaps the rows for "‹ Set sequencer" (back) and the seven kinds, in the same panel at the same top-left, so it needs no room beside the menu. A trackpad still opens it beside, on hover.
  • Dismissal: a tap or press outside closes it and does nothing else, so it adds no bar and changes no selection. Scrolling the lanes or the page, a rotation, the tab losing focus, or Escape on a hardware keyboard close it. A second long press elsewhere closes this menu and, at 450 ms, opens one for the new spot: one gesture.

Desktop and tablet: one component

The same on both

  • The items, their order, labels, disabled reasons and the header line (the desktop mockup's "Decided" list).
  • The panel: .knob-cc-menu's background, border, shadow and separators.
  • The previews themselves (dashed box, loop brace, split line).
  • Flip-then-clamp at the window's edges.

What differs when a finger opens it

  • Rows 44 px, not 32; labels 15 px, not 12; at least 260 px wide, not 220.
  • The keyboard tags (⌘X, ⌘C, ⌘D, ⌫, ⌥-click) are hidden. Tags that say why or how much ("nothing copied", "already fits", "1 bar", "all notes") stay.
  • It opens beside the finger and under the pressed lane, not down-right of the pointer.
  • The preview shows on the row under a sliding finger, not on hover.
  • Set sequencer ▸ drills in, not out to the side.
  • It opens after a 450 ms hold, not on right-click; a tap outside is consumed.

The size is chosen by what opened the menu, not by the screen's width, so it is zoom-safe and the tablet with a trackpad gets the desktop menu on a right-click (the "Opened by" switch).

Decided

  1. Row height and type: 44 px rows with 15 px labels on touch.
  2. The preview without hover: slide-to-choose; the lit row shows its preview, and lifting runs it.
  3. Which side the menu opens on: left of the finger, flipping right near the left edge. No handedness setting.
  4. The touch drag threshold: touch and pen drags on the lanes start at 8 px (LONG_PRESS.slopPx); the mouse keeps 4 px (SONG_DRAG_THRESHOLD_PX).
  5. What sizes the menu: the opening press's pointerType (touch or pen), not @media (pointer: coarse).
  6. Keyboard tags on touch: hidden; reason and amount tags stay.
  7. Set sequencer ▸: drills in, with a back row.
  8. Hold feedback: a ring around the finger that fills over the 450 ms, drawn only after 150 ms so a tap never flashes it.

Decided by the desktop mockup (song-region-menu-mockup.html, its "Decided" list): the menu's contents, Fit region to pattern, Paste's label and its disabled state, the header line, desktop's 32 px rows and Set sequencer's behavior. None is reopened here.
