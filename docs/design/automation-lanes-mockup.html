Automation lanes

Each part folds out lanes beneath it. A lane is a curve over song time that moves one of that part's parameters. Try the three tools on any lane, stamp a shape over a range, and press Play to watch the values and the locked knobs follow. Nothing here makes sound.

Knobs under automation

The Pad's Parts-tab knobs while the song plays. Cutoff and LFO 1 rate have lanes and are locked. Resonance has none and drags freely. Send A has a lane that is switched off, so it is free too. Two looks to choose from.

What to pick from this mockup

  1. Shape panel: A floats over the lane beside the selected range; B docks below the song like the detail pane.
  2. Locked knob: A lights the ring in the lane's colour with an AUTO tag; B dims the knob and adds a padlock.
  3. Lane height: S, M or L, and whether it is one height for every lane or each lane can be dragged taller.
  4. Lane colour by kind: teal for mixer, violet for inserts, amber for the voice, as drawn here. The alternative is the part's own colour.
  5. Gestures: click to add, drag a point to move it, drag the line to bend it, double-click a point to delete it, Alt-click a line to straighten it.
