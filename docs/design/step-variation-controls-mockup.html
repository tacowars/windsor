What changes

  1. Skip goes from both devices, and its Skip lane from the Song tab (windsor#935 decision 7: the migration drops skipChance and its seq.skipChance lanes). The Figure keeps its Skip.
  2. Randomness and Probability are two knobs of today's kind (the 52 × 56 px sequencer knob, 30 px dial, the part's teal), 0 to 1 shown as 0% to 100%, default 0%. They take the column Skip had: on the Grid, rows 1 and 2 of the last knob column; on the Arp, rows 2 and 3 under Gate. Each knob writes its field into the selected region's pattern, as Skip did.
  3. Lanes. Both are automatable: seq.randomness and seq.probability join the Song tab's + Add lane list under Sequencer, on a Grid or an Arp part. A knob whose lane is on is drawn locked as Skip is today: the sequencer-lane pink arc and pin, the AUTO tag, the lane's value at the playhead, no input. The Arp frames show the example song's Skip lane as a Probability lane (30% to 60%, read 30% at bar 1). Untick Arp Probability lane on above to see the knob free.
  4. Rests sound and Octave are two switches of the Arp's Retrigger kind (a 22 px off | on, labelled above), both off by default, in one column of their own after the knobs. The column has three rows like a knob column, so each switch sits level with a knob: rows 1 and 2 on the Grid (beside Randomness and Probability), rows 2 and 3 on the Arp. Click them.
  5. Reseed on the Grid. The Grid's first column gains the Arp's Seed field with Reseed as an icon beside it, so it reads Step, Seed, Randomize, Reverse, as the Arp's reads Retrigger, Seed, Randomize, Reverse. The field shows and edits the part's seed; the icon draws a new one and restarts the part's stream (the Arp's title: "A new seed: the part restarts its random stream now").
  6. Reseed in the region menu. A Grid region's menu lists Randomize, Reverse, Reseed, then Fit region to pattern, the order the Basslead's menu has. The menu grows 32 px. Hover an item.
  7. What the cells show does not change: the playhead and the cells show the steps as written, not what a varied step played (decision 3).

To decide

  1. The name for Randomness. Randomize already sits on both cards and rerolls the written pattern once; "Randomness" beside it reads like its amount. Alternatives: Stray (how far a step strays, the engine's own word), Drift, Vary. Recommended: Stray. It fits the knob, and it says the step moves away from what is written.
  2. The name for Probability. Alternatives: Chance, Often, Odds. Recommended: Chance, which fits the knob and reads with Stray as "how far, how often".
  3. The name for Rests sound. Alternatives: Rhythm, Flip rests. Recommended: Rests sound, since it says what turning it on allows.
  4. The name for the Octave switch. Both devices already have an Octave knob, the register, and the Arp has Octaves too, so a third "Octave" is ambiguous. Alternatives: By octave, Oct jumps. Recommended: By octave.
  5. Where the switches sit. Drawn: one column of their own after the knobs, the two switches one above the other and level with the two amount knobs. A row of two 112 px switches would cost 237 px of width instead of 98 to 125.
  6. The portrait tablet's sideways scroll (see The numbers). Proposed as drawn, since the rack already scrolls a long Grid. The other way is to fold the switches under the Step picker on narrow screens, which is a second layout to build and test.
  7. The lane readout for both amounts shows a percent (30%), as the knob does. Today's Skip lane shows 0.30.
