What changes

  1. Skip goes from both devices, and its Skip lane from the Song tab (windsor#935 decision 7: the migration drops skipChance and its seq.skipChance lanes). The Figure keeps its Skip.
  2. Vary and Chance are two knobs of today's kind (the 52 × 56 px sequencer knob, 30 px dial, the part's teal), 0 to 1 shown as 0% to 100%, default 0%. Vary is the engine's randomness, how far a step that varies strays; Chance is its probability, how often a step varies. They take the column Skip had: on the Grid, rows 1 and 2 of the last knob column; on the Arp, rows 2 and 3 under Gate. Each knob writes its field into the selected region's pattern, as Skip did.
  3. Lanes. Both are automatable: seq.randomness and seq.probability join the Song tab's + Add lane list under Sequencer, on a Grid or an Arp part, named Vary and Chance there and in the lane row. A knob whose lane is on is drawn locked as Skip is today: the sequencer-lane pink arc and pin, the AUTO tag, the lane's value at the playhead, no input. Every frame shows the Arp part's Skip lane as a Chance lane (30% to 60%, read 30% at bar 1), and the Arp frames its locked knob. Untick Arp Chance lane on above to see the knob free.
  4. Rests sound and Octave are two glyph buttons that toggle, both off by default. They are drawn as the app draws a toggled icon button (.seq-icon with aria-pressed, as the Euclid rail's lane-view buttons): off, a faint glyph on the panel; on, the glyph in the part's teal on the inset. In the Play section they take Reseed's 22 px size and its always-drawn border. Each has a tooltip of one sentence, and its name as its accessible name. Hover a button for its tooltip, click it to toggle. The example has the Grid's Octave on and the Arp's Rests sound on.
    • Rests sound: "Rests sound: a varied step may also go silent or sound where a rest was written".
    • Octave: "Octave: pitch strays by whole octaves of the step's own note instead of borrowing another step's".
  5. Where the glyph buttons sit (recommended, By the knobs): on the Grid, side by side at the foot of the Vary and Chance column, in its third row, which is empty today, so the Grid keeps today's width. On the Arp that column is full (Gate, Vary, Chance), so the two buttons take a 22 px column beside it, Rests sound level with Vary and Octave level with Chance. The other way, On the rail in the picker above, puts them on the device's rail between its name and the region badge, the place the Euclid's lane-view toggle has, at the rail's 18 px: no width on either device, but a smaller target and away from the knobs they shape.
  6. Reseed on the Grid. The Grid's first column gains the Arp's Seed field with Reseed as an icon beside it, so it reads Step, Seed, Randomize, Reverse, as the Arp's reads Retrigger, Seed, Randomize, Reverse. The field shows and edits the part's seed; the icon draws a new one and restarts the part's stream (the Arp's title: "A new seed: the part restarts its random stream now").
  7. Reseed in the region menu. A Grid region's menu lists Randomize, Reverse, Reseed, then Fit region to pattern, the order the Basslead's menu has. The menu grows 32 px. Hover an item.
  8. What the cells show does not change: the playhead and the cells show the steps as written, not what a varied step played (decision 3).

The numbers

Measured in the app, in place, on the portrait tablet: the knob label at 8.5 px Chakra Petch with 0.1 em letter spacing, set to each name on the Grid's Skip knob. Vary is 24.3 px and Chance 38.3 px, both inside the 52 px knob; today's widest knob label, Acc mod, is 42.9 px. Chance leaves 15 px to Acc vel's label beside it, so no knob column widens. In the lane row, at 11 px, Vary is 29.7 px and Chance 47.0 px, inside today's 78 px name box.

The width each placement costs was measured the same way: a pair of 22 px buttons 3 px apart put in the third row of the Grid's Skip column left the device at its 965 px box, and a 22 px column put after the Arp's Gate and Skip column grew that device by 24 px (its 2 px gap and the column).

Decided

tacowars's picks on this mockup's first round, recorded in docs/log/2026-10-24-step-variation-controls.md: the knobs and their lanes are Vary and Chance, and Rests sound and Octave are glyph buttons that toggle, with the tooltips above, not switches.

To decide

  1. Where the glyph buttons sit. Recommended: by the knobs, as drawn, so each sits beside the amounts it shapes; on the Grid it costs no width. The other way: on the rail, no width on either device, at 18 px.
  2. The glyphs, switched with the pickers above.
    • Rests sound: Rest sign (recommended: the eighth-rest sign, which names what the button is about) or Hit and gap (a solid bar beside a dashed one: a hit and a rest that may trade places).
    • Octave: 8 and arrows (recommended: the octave's 8 with an arrow up and an arrow down) or Octave span (an arrow both ways between two lines, an interval).
  3. The portrait tablet's sideways scroll (see The numbers). The Grid no longer scrolls. The Arp scrolls 11 px with the buttons by the knobs. Proposed as drawn, since the rack already scrolls a long Grid; putting the buttons on the rail removes it.
  4. The lane readout for both amounts shows a percent (30%), as the knob does. Today's Skip lane shows 0.30.
