What changes

  1. Both views start below the console header and fill the rest of the window: the header keeps its two rows (tabs, CPU, undo and redo, the transport; the part chips) and stays live. The view's padding is today's 24 px at the sides and bottom and 16 px under the header.
  2. Nothing else moves inside either view. Today's controls, in today's places, at today's values. Each canvas is sized from its own CSS box × devicePixelRatio, so the roll shows fewer rows at its 15 px/row zoom and Spectre's lanes are shorter (about 85 px less at 1440 × 900).
  3. No Undo or Redo in the workbench header. Record 2026-10-17-spectre-drawn-cells decision 10 drew them there; the build left them out, and they are not added: the console's Undo and Redo sit just above. Cmd/Ctrl-Z and Shift-Cmd/Ctrl-Z keep working.
  4. Switching tabs does not close a view. Another tab shows its page as it does today, with nothing of the view on it. The view belongs to the tab its card lives on (Song for both examples here; Mixer for a Spectre on the master or a send), and going back to that tab shows it still expanded, as it was left. Only Close (the roll's rail button) and Esc close it.
