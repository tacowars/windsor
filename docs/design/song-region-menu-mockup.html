Right-click menu on the part lanes

A right-click on a region (a long press on touch, 450 ms as the part color picker's) selects it, as a click does today, and opens this menu at the pointer. A right-click on a gap selects the part and opens the empty-lane menu. The lanes are today's dev build: five parts made in the browser after the old-format song files were refused (see the notes). Hover an item to see what it would do.

What the menu holds

  • Region menu: Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Delete and Split here; Zoom to region and Loop region; the part's pattern actions, the ones its card offers today; Bounce part to Roll… and Part color…. Paste reads "Paste at bar N" and lands at the right-clicked bar, rounded down; Split here cuts at the nearest bar line (splitRegion's snap). ⌘V still pastes at the playhead, so the menu's Paste carries no ⌘V hint. Paste is disabled with its reason when nothing is copied or the clip is another kind. Menu text is US English (Color, Quantize).
  • Pattern actions by kind, from the cards: Grid has Randomize and Reverse and no Reseed (gridControls.ts). Arp, Basslead and Figure add Reseed (arpCard.ts, bassCard.ts, figureControls.ts). Euclid has Capture or Release (euclidPatternPage.ts). Roll has Quantize, named with the card's Snap ("to 1/16"; disabled at Snap Off). Fit region to pattern sets the region to one pass of its pattern, the cycle its block draws (CYCLE_TICKS), on every kind that has one, Chord included; Arp, a Figure canon and an empty pattern don't get it. Chord's card has only Delete last, so a Chord region's pattern section holds Fit region to pattern alone.
  • Bounce part to Roll… follows bounceButtonState: it isn't shown on a Roll or a no-sequencer part. A part with no regions has no region menu, so it is never drawn disabled here.
  • The no-sequencer lane is drawn as it is today: a new part gets one whole-song region (∞, "no sequencer"), and its lane can be split, drawn on, copied and pasted like any other. Its region menu has the edits, Zoom and Loop, and Set sequencer ▸ where the pattern actions go. The submenu lists KIND_LABELS without None and calls setSequencerKindLive.
  • Placement: the menu opens at the pointer. If it would cross the window's edge it flips above or to the left, then clamps, as songRowMenuPlacement.ts does for the ⋯ menu. The submenu opens beside its row and flips left near the right edge. The panel, border, shadow, head and dim right-aligned tags are .knob-cc-menu's. Rows are 32 px on desktop. The only touch device in scope is tacowars's 12.9-inch tablet; its row height is set by the tablet mockup, which follows this one.
  • Data: songs/escalera.json and songs/clicks.json are song format 3, and this build reads 16, so both were refused. The frame is built from a song made in the dev build at 16 bars, named after escalera's parts: acid (Grid), kick (Euclid), lead (Roll, 4 notes on a 3-bar loop in its first region), arp (Arp) and pad (no sequencer).

Decided

  1. Fit region to pattern: on every region whose pattern has a cycle, the one its block draws its ticks from (CYCLE_TICKS): Roll, Grid, Euclid, Basslead, a non-empty Chord, and a Figure (its schedule, else its line). Set to one pass. Not on Arp, a Figure canon or an empty pattern; a Chord region's pattern group holds Fit alone.
  2. Paste with nothing copied or a clip of another kind: disabled, its reason in the tag.
  3. Paste's label: "Paste at bar N", no ⌘V hint.
  4. Header line: kept.
  5. Row height: 32 px on desktop; the 12.9-inch tablet is mocked in song-region-menu-tablet-mockup.html.
  6. Set sequencer: does what the Parts tab's Seq picker does (setSequencerKindLive), Roll's case included.
