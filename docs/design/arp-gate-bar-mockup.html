What changes

  1. A Gate row in the Arp's step cells, between Oct and A. A note cell draws a bar: a horizontal fill and the cell's own gate as text (1, .5, .25, .01), as the Figure's Vel bar draws velocity. A rest or a tie draws a blank cell, as for Oct, A and S.
  2. The fill is the played gate: the part's Gate (the knob, or the seq.gate lane where one plays) times the cell's gate, floored at GATE_MIN (0.01, audioConstants.ts). Move the Gate knob in the frame, or the slider above: every fill repaints and every text stays put.
  3. The text is the cell's gate, faint at 1 (no gate written, as the normaliser writes it). A drag sweeps 0 to 1 over 80 px in steps of 0.05, floored at 0.01, previews on the bar and writes once on release; a double-click puts the cell back to 1 (the field removed). The table below follows the cells as you drag.
  4. The head grows by 24 px (one 20 px row and its 4 px gap), from 126 to 150 px, inside the same 244 px device. Everything in the strip below Oct moves down 24 px: A, S, the ratchet, the lane names' corner (+ Lane, the lane count, Ratchet) and the lanes. The lanes' window shrinks from 90 to 66 px of the 218 px scroller: with this song's two lanes the first shows whole and 22 of the second's 42 px, and the strip scrolls, as it does today past two lanes.
  5. Basslead is unchanged. It draws the same cells (arpStepCells.ts), and the cell builder takes an option that leaves the row out, so its strip stays at five held rows (the Basslead frames show it, Today and Proposed alike).

To decide

  1. A cell at 1. Decision 2 draws a cell at 1 faint, as the Vel bar draws full velocity, and also has every fill show the played gate. The Vel bar at full hides its fill, which would leave a cell at 1 blank whatever the Gate knob says. Proposed: the text faint and the fill still drawn at the played gate, fainter (20% of the cell colour instead of 45%). The other look is under Cell at 1 above.
  2. A note that runs on. A note followed by a tie or a slide runs to the next onset whatever its gate (windsor#933 decision 3): here cell 3, before the tie, and cell 7, before cell 8's slide. Decision 2 still fills them to Gate × cell. The other look, under Held to next above, fills them to the end, hatched, so the bar shows what plays.
