Clip envelopes in the Roll

A roll carries up to four clip envelopes: voice automation that loops with the roll's loop and travels with its region, as in Ableton Live's clip view. The ENV toggle at the top of the keys column switches the note pane into envelope mode. The column then holds a picker of the voice targets, and the chosen target's envelope is drawn over the notes, which dim and stop responding. The Song tab's Edit, Draw and Shape tools (E D S) and its Shape popover edit it, on the roll's own Snap. Values run −1..1: the middle line is 0, the patch's own setting, and the readout shows the offset.

Each Roll below is live: try the toggle, the picker, the tools, and the Shape tool (drag a range across the pane). The curves are drawings. Everything but the toggle, the picker, the tools column and the curve is today's Roll, copied from the app.

Questions for tacowars

  1. The toggle's look and place. Drawn as a small ENV button at the top of the keys column, over the top two key rows in the device. The alternative is the corner above the keys, which today names the guide chord.
  2. The notes in envelope mode. Drawn at 30 % and inert, so the envelope reads against the music. The alternative is hiding them.
  3. The column widens. In envelope mode the keys column goes from 44 to 112 px and the keys give way to the picker, so target names fit. The alternative keeps 44 px and opens the picker only as a list over the pane.
  4. The tools column. Edit, Draw and Shape sit in a third column after the roll's two, with the tool's hint. The roll's controls are unchanged, and Snap stays the roll's.
  5. A bend handle. The Song tab has none: you drag the line. State 4 draws a stand-in diamond. Draw one here and on the Song tab, or keep the Song tab's way?
